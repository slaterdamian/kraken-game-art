Kraken Game Art

Shows the art of whatever game Discord says you're playing on your NZXT Kraken screen.

  1. Join the Lanyard Discord server (it's how this page can see your activity).
  2. In Discord: Settings → Activity Privacy → Share your detected activities on.
  3. Get your user ID: Settings → Advanced → Developer Mode, then right-click your name → Copy User ID.
  4. Fill in below, copy the URL, and paste it into NZXT CAM → Lighting / LCD → Web Integration.
Enter your user ID to start.
